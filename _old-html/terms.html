1. What Mandi 24x7 is

The vendor sells the goods, sets the price, and is responsible for their quality, weight, packing and delivery or pickup. We provide the app, the order system and the khata (ledger) tool.

3. Orders, prices and billing

  • Rates are for delivery on the date shown in the app. Orders placed before the city’s cutoff time are delivered on that date.
  • Fresh produce is sold by weight. The vendor weighs your items and your bill is based on the final weighed quantity, so it may differ slightly from the estimate. If an item is not available the vendor may supply less or none of it, and you are not charged for what you do not receive.
  • Delivery charges and other charges (for example a rain or small-order fee) are shown in the cart before you order.

4. Payments and khata

  • The khata is a record-keeping tool between you and the vendor. Any credit (udhaar) is given by the vendor, not by Mandi 24x7. Please pay your dues to the vendor on time.
